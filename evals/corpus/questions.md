## Admission

1. Who may sponsor an admission lease?

Whether any seat holder may sponsor, or sponsorship requires two seats.

**ANSWERED (fixture, 2026-02-01): any single seat.** Sponsorship is
revocable by majority vote, which is the real guard; requiring two seats
adds latency to the common case.

2. Lease term length.

Whether an admission lease runs for a fixed number of epochs or until
revoked. The renewal path differs between the two.

3. Observer state visibility.

Whether observers see full committee certificates or only their hashes while
their lease is pending.
